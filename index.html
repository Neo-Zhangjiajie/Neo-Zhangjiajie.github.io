Jiajie Zhang

I am a fourth-year Ph.D. student in the Department of Computer Science and Technology at Tsinghua University, advised by Prof. Ling Feng and Prof. Juanzi Li. I am also a research intern at Zhipu AI, where I am a core contributor to the GLM series of foundation models.

My research focuses on long-context large language models, long-horizon agents, and efficient reasoning. My goal is to make LLMs trustworthy, efficient, and robust on ultra-long, complex tasks.

I am currently working on scalable environments, feedback mechanisms, and reinforcement learning algorithms for long-horizon agents, toward agents that can keep learning and evolving online, and ultimately achieve recursive self-improvement (RSI).
